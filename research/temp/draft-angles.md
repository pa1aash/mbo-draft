# Step 10 — the three analytical angles

The deliverable's structure is FIXED (five H2 headings from `required_section_headings`), so the
angles differ in **analytical stance**, not shape. Each draft answers the same five sections from
a different reading of what the evidence means.

## Draft A — PROSECUTION (strongest-thesis)
The paper's scholarship is unreliable and the fixes are severe. Argue the mandatory items
forcefully and in descending severity: a fabricated numeric threshold attributed to Demsar; a
citation of Fan that inverts the source's own convergence theorem; SNGP cited to bound a claim
its Figure 1 contradicts; a false claim about Li/Rudner/Wilson's K-range; Rahaman cited to
motivate the axis it calls weak; Abe cited for a sweep it never ran. Treat N6's survival as
narrow and contingent — it rests on scoping, one of its three grounds was falsified on contact,
and one paywalled lead remains unchecked. The thesis: a paper this careful with its numbers
should not be this careless with its sources, and the gap is itself the finding.

## Draft B — DEFENCE (steelman-contrarian)
The minority view, defended seriously: the paper is unusually rigorous and most of the audit is
cosmetic. Every quantitative claim checkable against a repo artifact reproduced — four corner
eta-squareds and CIs, Elimination 1's seven figures, the inversion counts, both frozen-cell
claims to the seed including "ten of sixteen". The mbo.py line traces resolve exactly. Engine
stamps are real. Zero undefined citations. Three confirmed year traps handled correctly. N6
survived a 2026 recency sweep, three near-miss re-confirmations, a ~347-paper forward-citation
walk, and an adversarial sweep of three previously-unsearched regions. Not one of the twelve
fixes touches a number the paper computed. The thesis: the audit found framing defects in a
paper whose evidence is sound, and a reviewer who conflates the two would be making an error.

## Draft C — SYNTHESIS (boundary conditions, practitioner-facing)
Both hold, and the useful question is which claims are load-bearing. Sound evidence, unsound
scholarship — so the fixes are cheap and none threatens a result. Focus on boundary conditions:
which findings survive which challenge (the interaction survives the floor-effect test but rides
on min-max; the raw-units attenuation survives normalization but not the floor-effect test; both
together are defensible, either alone is an overclaim). Organize around what the author should
actually do with limited hours before a deadline, ranked by value per hour. The thesis: the
audit's real output is a priority ordering, not a defect list.
