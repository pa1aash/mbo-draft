## Tension 1: The K-sweep is simultaneously N2's novelty engine and C's self-inflicted wound — and it is mis-framed either way
- **Locus L1** ([[interim-report-k-and-finite-width-artifact-vs-class-property]]) commits: the N5 "K=2≫K=5 proves a finite-width artifact" argument is a **category error** — ensemble cardinality K ≠ per-member width n (each member is a fixed HID=96 2-layer MLP; NTK/spectral bias is a width→∞ statement that never mentions ensembles). The K-decline runs *backward* from Lakshminarayanan/L/R/W, best read as small-K σ-estimation noise. N2 (ranking flip by K) = **NONE FOUND** but pre-empted in *strength* by L/R/W's K-robustness (Fig A.7).
- **The cross-locus dynamic:** the single result the paper leans on for N2 novelty is the same result it (mis)uses as N5 mechanism evidence. It cannot do both jobs: as N2 evidence it is a genuine (if fragile) novelty; as N5 evidence it is a category error a Bayesian-DL reviewer will catch instantly.
- **How the draft should engage this:** the N2 verdict (NONE FOUND) stands, but the report must explicitly *decline* to present the K-sweep as finite-width evidence, and name the missing experiment — a **width ablation (sweep HID at fixed K)** — as what N5 actually demands. Confidence ~65% (L1), so hedge the σ-noise reading.

## Tension 2: The σ-mechanism dies from both directions — which kills C's σ-story but redirects (not refutes) C toward the posterior mean
- **Locus L2** ([[interim-report-n4-sigma-mechanism-scope-and-ovadia]]) commits: the raw distance-aware mechanism is **PRIOR WORK FOUND** (SNGP/DUQ); the "LCB + growing GP variance = implicit trust region" synthesis is **NONE FOUND** but *contradicted* by the paper's own β=0 control (gap survives σ-removal) and by TuRBO (which had to build an explicit TR because unchecked variance growth is a problem, not a free mechanism). The DUE scope-catch **holds in the paper's favor** (DUE's caveat is deep-kernel-specific; the paper uses vanilla Matérn).
- **The cross-locus dynamic:** L2's β=0 result ("the edge is the posterior mean, not σ") and L1's "it's not K-as-width either" **converge** on the same target — the GP's posterior *mean smoothness*, not its uncertainty. So N4 does not refute candidate C; it *relocates* C's mechanism from σ-calibration to mean-smoothness. But that relocation removes C's easiest evidence (σ) and leaves it dependent on manipulation it has not run.
- **How the draft should engage this:** N4 verdict = mechanism partly owned (SNGP), offline-MBO-LCB-TR synthesis unclaimed; and C's viable mechanism is mean-smoothness, for which σ-based controls are irrelevant. Flag the β=0 *numbers* as NOT VERIFIABLE (repro gave 0.504→0.511, not the cited 0.51→0.47).

## Tension 3: C has the highest novelty ceiling (N7 "first at all") and the weakest current evidence (manipulation unrun, K-evidence miscategorized)

- **The cross-locus dynamic:** L4 gives C the strongest possible novelty verdict, but L1 + L2 show the paper's *current* mechanism evidence is a category error (K) plus a dead σ-channel — i.e., the manipulation that would earn N7's novelty (smooth the net M1, roughen the GP M2) is, per the repo's own MECHANISM_EXPERIMENTS.md, planned-but-unrun. - **How the draft should engage this:** state C's one-sentence as genuinely novel *contingent on running the bidirectional manipulation*; contrast with A/D, whose contributions do not depend on unrun experiments. This is the "C is a bet" finding.
